# Rubric — deck

Inputs to read: `ideas/alignment.md`, `ideas/proposal.md`, `results/summary.md`, `figures/`, `research-map.md`, `company-context.md`, the rendered deck (PDF or images).

1. **Every claim traces.** For each numeric or comparative claim on a slide, find its line in `summary.md` (with run ids) or the digest (`[reported]`). A number with no source is a blocker; a paper's number shown as our result is a blocker.
2. **Labels kept.** `[reported]`, `[measured]`, `[company-context]`, `[inference]` distinctions survive on the slides (visually or in notes).
3. **Ground point on one slide.** The paper → company bridge (shared structure, bridging concept, what changes) is stated as in `alignment.md`, not replaced by a vaguer story.
4. **Hypothesis verdict shown.** Including negative or partial results; a deck that omits the falsification outcome is a blocker.
5. **Where it does not help is present.**
6. **Ask is concrete.** The decision requested cites `company-context §6` (who decides, what evidence they trust) and the research map's win definition.
7. **Figures legible.** Rendered check: axes, legends, CI visible at slide size; no figure re-drawn by hand with different numbers.
8. **Confidentiality.** No public-search terms mixed with internal metrics in a way that would leak if the deck were shared outside.
